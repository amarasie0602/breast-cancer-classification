Breast Cancer Histopathology Classifier

Upload a breast histology image for a 3-stage prediction: image validation, benign/malignant classification, and (for malignant results) cancer subtype.

Research and educational purposes only — not a medical diagnosis. This is a research/coursework project demonstrating an MLOps pipeline. It has not undergone clinical validation and must not be used to inform real patient care or treatment decisions. "Benign" here means a non-cancerous tumor, not confirmed-healthy tissue — this dataset contains no normal/healthy tissue images at all, only benign and malignant tumor specimens. The model does not estimate ER, PR, HER2, or triple-negative status.
Click to choose a file, or drag one here
Subtype prediction is not statistically validated. BreakHis contains only 5 lobular, 6 papillary and 9 mucinous carcinoma patients in total, so a patient-level split leaves about one patient per class to validate against — far too few to measure subtype accuracy meaningfully. Treat this as a demonstration of the pipeline, not a reliable subtype call. Only these four subtypes exist in the dataset; DCIS, LCIS, inflammatory, Paget, metaplastic, cribriform and tubular carcinoma are not represented, and an image of one of those would still be forced into one of the four.
