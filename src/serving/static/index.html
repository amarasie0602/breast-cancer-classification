What the labels do and don't mean

“Benign” means a non-cancerous tumour, not healthy tissue. Every image in this dataset comes from a tumour biopsy — it contains no normal tissue at all, so the model has never seen a healthy sample. The model also does not estimate ER, PR, HER2 or triple-negative status, which require immunohistochemistry rather than H&E morphology.

Malignant subtype
Not statistically validated. BreakHis contains only 5 lobular, 6 papillary and 9 mucinous carcinoma patients in total, leaving roughly one patient per class to validate against. Only these four subtypes exist in the dataset — DCIS, LCIS, inflammatory, Paget, metaplastic, cribriform and tubular carcinoma are not represented, and an image of one of those would still be forced into one of the four.
